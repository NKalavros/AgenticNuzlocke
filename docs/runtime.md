# Runtime reference

Read [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) first. This file contains the detailed controller contract.

## Runtime loop (truth)

Three systems. **System 1** (Jev + code) selects button sequences, with fixed preparation menus handled by code; the Action Arbiter validates all button proposals. **System 2** (the vision director, `system2` in `config/agents.yaml`) is called only when there is a decision to make: it sets the next objective after reading System 1's journal, and it writes conditional plans for trainer battles. Plans refresh on opponent species/level, active party slot, status, critical HP, exhausted moves, or eligible-roster changes. **System 3** (the deterministic rules controller, spanning `system3.py`, `policy.py`, the referee and ledger) establishes constraints before decisions; see System 3 below.

### System 3: the Nuzlocke rules

**No battle items**, except capture balls (Poké, Great, Ultra) for catching. Healing is Pokémon Centers or Mom only. Audited Rare Candy preparation is enabled: healthy parties receive the exact deficit and consume candies through normal menus (Bulbasaur in the lab to level 8, Viridian Center to 12, Pewter Center to 12 before Brock; supported Centers to 18 after Boulder; Cerulean Center to 19). These routine targets apply `cap − level_cap_buffer − 1`; the final leader top-up below uses the full 14/21 cap. Grants and uses are recorded. Fresh boots select verified Bulbasaur and SET battle style.

Evolution-family duplicates reroll once balls have ever been acquired. Encounter outcomes cannot be overwritten by later fights. Stable capture identities retain permanent deaths across healing, evolution, and party order changes. Caps apply at battle entry; levels gained within that battle are allowed. The final arbiter checks menu choices against a fresh non-advancing snapshot.

Acceptance: `uv run nuzlocke benchmark --trials 5 --steps 1500 --output runs/brock-acceptance.json`. Fresh private servers only; retain failed trials. Diagnostic savestate replays are not acceptance trials.

- **Catch**: the first eligible wild encounter per area (numeric map ID, except Mt. Moon floors 59–61 share one slot) after balls have ever been acquired gets ITEM → the Poké Ball row, without asking Jev. The activation stays latched even when balls run out. Ever-owned evolution families reroll; other encounter outcomes are permanently caught or forfeited. "Give a nickname?" is answered NO. Routes 1/22/2 and Forest searches persist until resolution; missing early slots trigger backtracking before Pewter’s east exit. Routes 3/4, Mt. Moon, and Routes 24/25 are also scheduled. Search counts are diagnostic, never a timeout permission to leave. ROM land tables containing only owned families defer the slot without consuming it. Fishing, inaccessible areas, and gifts are not comprehensively scheduled yet.
- **Encounter approach versus pacing**: when grass is unreachable on the observed walking graph, choose a reference/observed grass destination and use directed ledge routing; do not replace that destination with generic exploration. Only `encounter_phase=pacing` on known encounter terrain suppresses recovery. Route 5's side lanes cannot reach the central grass against the ledges: when no local grass route exists but the north exit is reachable, return to Cerulean, cross its southern apron to `(23,35)`, and re-enter Route 5 at `(13,0)`. Observed Route 5 grass retains this pending detour across maps/checkpoints; a resolved or duplicate-only encounter releases it. This measured detour replaces the side-lane explore oscillation, without consuming the encounter. Story, encounter and healing rules share Route 4’s east-region boundary at the B1F exit (x=24); the previous x>25 cutoff flipped its own exit apron back toward Mt. Moon.
- **Field items** (`knowledge/items.py`): pick up reachable item balls before encounter searches and story routes; healing, storage, preparation, supply and battle interventions retain priority. The server reads item IDs from `wMapSpriteExtraData` (`D504`, slot−1 pairs) and labels them in goals, journal and the route dashboard. Ordinary field items accept A from any reachable adjacent side; starters/fossils keep their separate scripted interactions. Hidden/collected objects are excluded; a full bag, unreachable approach or three failed interactions defers pickup. No automatic tossing, hidden-item sweep or cross-map collection is implemented. Item restrictions in battle are unchanged. Grass destinations use genuinely reachable tiles, not the door-only blocked-destination exception; all available NPCs and uncollected balls remain obstacles at their latest live coordinates even off screen. Scrolling past the trainer blocking Route 25 TM19 must not make the enclosure reachable or replace the Bill objective; a moved/hidden NPC releases its previous tile. Cached routes include the destination coordinate so a new grass target invalidates an old route.
- **Run**: flee wild duplicates, non-capture wild encounters when candy preparation is enabled, or a wild battle with the active Pokémon below 25% HP. For an eligible first encounter with balls, first switch to a healthy legal teammate that passes the existing level/status/PP/matchup checks; flee if none qualifies. Actor and arbiter require the same replacement. A trainer battle cannot be fled.
- **Heal**: any living member below 50% HP, with a status problem, or with all moves exhausted triggers a route to a Center or Mom. Nurse menus accept YES or Red Star's HEAL. Route 5 and the north–south Underground Path route forward through Route 6 to Vermilion's Center; Vermilion, Rock Tunnel, Lavender, Celadon and Fuchsia Centers use the nurse target. Unknown routes retain a mandatory healing objective for System 2 instead of falling back to story or encounter searches. Supported Centers permit audited candy deficits when preparation calls for them; these routes do not add PC deposit automation. Before Brock, restore full HP/status and observed PP, including after the junior trainer. Heal the whole party before candy grants. Poison can faint outside battle; healing routes are still movement, not immunity.
- **Storage**: permanent deaths survive healing. Route dead party members to a Center PC, face `(13,4)` upward, and use SOMEONE'S/BILL'S PC → DEPOSIT. Deterministic deposit menus currently support Viridian and Pewter Centers. This is not general team/box management.
- **Shop** (`agents/shop.py`): below ten total capture balls and with ¥200+, city supply beats visit supported Marts. Read the full live `wItemList` (CF7B count/IDs/FF), gated by visible BUY UI; select the best affordable **Ultra → Great → Poké Ball**, including off-screen rows. Buy only the deficit to ten, bounded by funds. Quantity input has no filled cursor: handle × before hollow-menu paging. Verify selected item, displayed unit price, final total, and bag/money delta; retain pending purchase in checkpoints. Exit through B and QUIT. Price/result mismatches or 60 transaction cycles pause. Actual Cerulean stock currently has only Poké Balls; richer inventories are covered by tests.
- **Wipe**: the whole party at 0 HP, or the blackout warp home with a healed party, ends the run (#29).
- **XP buffer and lead rotation** (`agents/level_buffer.py`, `lead_rotation.py`): `level_cap_buffer: 1` reserves cap−1 and above for necessity. Rotate before encounters via START → POKéMON → SWITCH; verify by capture identity and close all menus before movement. Pending rotation survives checkpoints. Battle alternatives must be legal, below the reserve band, at least 60% HP, status OK, within three levels of the enemy, have usable damaging PP, and have no weakness to the enemy’s species types. Legal catch/flee choices precede an optional switch; if no suitable alternative exists, a legal reserved member can fight. Hard-ineligible members still must switch or flee. Outside battle, an illegal lead is replaced even if the only legal alternative is reserved. These are heuristics, not damage calculations or guarantees against excess XP; entering battle then switching can still share XP. A rotation with 40 cycles without menu progress pauses for inspection. The cursor matcher tolerates omitted gender glyphs in Nidoran names; slot position and capture identity still determine the target.
- **Off-screen candy rows** (`agents/inventory.py`): match the visible highlighted item to full bag order and step toward Rare Candy one observation at a time. Use the same forced action in preparation and final policy; do not offer B just because the candy row is below the screen. Picking up the Nugget made candy the fifth item in the watched run. Preparation pauses after 60 menu attempts without a verified level increase; the counter restores with checkpoints and resets on observed candy level progress.
- **Off-screen capture balls** (`agents/inventory.py`): the same full-bag scroller owns battle inventory selection in System 1 and the arbiter. Match the visible highlighted label to inventory order, scroll one row and reobserve, then press A only on the chosen positive-quantity capture ball. Canonical names and ordinary capture-ball IDs 1–4 authorize selection; a BALL substring alone does not. The helper first applies survival, duplicate and encounter rules: trainer/resolved/duplicate captures, unavailable balls and a required switch/flee close the bag through B. Generic menu fallback cannot select another item or cancel an authorized scroll. Fresh policy checks replace stale throw proposals with the currently required scroll or survival exit. No separate capture cursor state is cached across checkpoints, and scratch menu index/scroll RAM cannot override visible labels. The Route 9 regression had Poké Balls at bag slot ten, below four visible non-ball rows; the fix captured the boxed Ekans in a private replay.
- **Final leader preparation** (`agents/gym_preparation.py`): once the leader is the next task, persist heal → prepare → challenge in the paired controller checkpoint. Start at the unbeaten leader interaction tile for Brock `(54,4,2)`, Misty `(65,4,3)`, Surge `(92,5,2)`, Erika `(134,4,4)`, or Koga `(157,4,11)`. For Koga, first arrival in Fuchsia city, Center or gym starts preparation before the maze trainers. With the verified Surge helper, open locks also establish readiness in Vermilion Gym, city, or Center. Require HEAL/YES acceptance at the nurse counter, full living HP/status/observed maximum PP, and closed text before releasing healing. The primary objective stays code-owned across planner updates and route/field-move subgoals. At the corresponding Center, grant only the exact candy deficit and use normal menus to reach the current cap (Surge 24, Erika 29, Koga 43), bypassing the routine travel buffer. Leader interaction A is blocked until readiness; leaving to heal remains legal. Return damage sends the stage back to healing. Only the observed badge retires the sequence. Move-learning/evolution protections and permanent deaths remain active; over-cap members remain ineligible. The endpoint uses configured caps and observed badge count for approved Centers/lab or exact unbeaten leader positions.
- **Briefing** (`constraints` for System 1 and System 2): the rules, the level cap, the next boss, and the known trainers on this map with their teams, from `nuzlocke/knowledge/trainers_red.yaml`. That file includes vanilla early trainers, the S.S. Anne rival, Surge and Erika: Red Star may differ, and the RAM enemy wins in battle.

System 2 first plans each **trainer battle** at its first menu (trigger `trainer battle`). Both systems receive major status and shared `battle.move_facts`: effect/category, vanilla baseline power/accuracy, observed type/PP, and unavailability reasons. The planner returns `battle_plan`: `opening_moves`, ranked `moves`, `switch_to`, `switch_below`, `notes`, `move_rules`, and `avoid_moves`. Example rule: `{"move":"SLEEP POWDER","enemy_status":["OK"],"reason":"Sleep once, then attack"}`. Allowed statuses are OK/SLP/PSN/BRN/FRZ/PAR/UNKNOWN; missing status is UNKNOWN. System 1 receives the whole battle plan plus the plan's intent and `do_not` on every battle choice. Free-text notes remain advisory; structured conditions and bans gate choices, openers, and fallback. Malformed conditions invalidate the plan instead of making it unconditional. Openers count only after an observed PP decrease. A nonempty `moves` list is an allowlist for repeatable choices: an opening Growl disappears after its prescribed uses unless also listed in `moves`. This prevents Jev from continuing two opening Growls indefinitely. Changes to enemy major status refresh the plan; sleep-counter ticks do not. The plan is dropped when battle ends.

Field Log groups reasoning events (including DIRECTOR and planner streams) into one THINK block until the final action DECIDE or ACT. Director mode/ownership announcements are reasoning, so they do not prematurely split the block. The repo's `environment/dashboard.py` compatibility-checked override serves `field_log.js` without changing the installed dependency. Live and replay rendering share this grouping. Routine director routing and context-token bookkeeping are hidden, role prefixes appear only implicitly in the group, and raw response JSON is not rendered as thinking. Cursor thinking deltas are buffered through the throttle, flushed at completion, and sent as cumulative `[role:thinking]` snapshots; the UI replaces the block text rather than stacking duplicate snapshots. Existing fragmented legacy events can be joined but missing/truncated text cannot be recovered. The JavaScript helper is read on each dashboard request, so presentation changes can be loaded without restarting gameplay; provider/gameplay code requires a new process.

### Integrity and server ownership

- `GET /snapshot` is serialized with actions and reads without advancing frames. The adapter's transition hook tracks outcomes after every internal action, including wait frames and dialog paging.
- The arbiter validates semantic menu choices with `agents/policy.py` against a fresh snapshot. Dead/ineligible switches, exhausted moves, disallowed items, and actions after a wipe are rejected.
- Newly launched servers require the controller's token for actions, loads, new games, and preparation. A competing writer receives HTTP 409. Dashboard reads and START/PAUSE/STOP remain available.
- `checkpoints/pending.json` is written before mutation; an immutable state/controller pair and atomic `current.json` are published after each completed cycle, including battle cycles. Never delete a pending marker to force legal resume.
- `events.jsonl` chains ledger commits, candy grants/uses, wipes, and milestones; resume checks its head against SQLite and the paired controller. This is local integrity checking, not an externally anchored audit.
- `checkpoint.enabled` / `every_steps` are legacy settings: the active paired-commit path is unconditional per completed cycle. Mid-action crashes may refuse resume to avoid replaying an outcome; use an isolated diagnostic copy.

### Cadence (`config/run.yaml`)

- **The emulator is not real time.** pokemon-agent runs PyBoy headless and only advances frames inside `/action` (`press_*` and `walk_*` are 20 frames each, `wait_N` is N). Nothing happens between cycles, so waiting in Python does not let text print or a scene settle — send wait frames in the action instead
- `prompt_interval_s: 0.1` — minimum wall time per cycle (`NUZLOCKE_PROMPT_INTERVAL_S`). It only paces the run for a human watching
- `press_interval_s: 0.1` — tiny gap between buttons in a burst
- `max_actions_per_proposal: 12` — logical actions; `walk_*_2`…`_5` macros count as one
- **On ordinary floor one `walk_*` press is one tile**, from any facing. A press into a wall only turns the player. Gen 1 has no turn-in-place, and the old second press after a turn moved two tiles (57 of 63 turning walks in run `20260927-235608-cc25eb`)
- **Paging text**: an open text box with no prompt is `skip_dialog`, checked before any planner look — a new box does not get a look first. Battle narration ("Enemy SQUIRTLE used TACKLE!") is paged the same way; the FIGHT menu and the move list do not read as a text box, so the mash stops at them. The planner is not called between lines. After four pages on one tile (B presses or `skip_dialog` mashes) the director stops calling it speech, so the beat stays the goal; only single B presses force a look every fourth page, since a mash already runs until the box closes or stalls. A YES/NO is not paged this way, and neither is a text box that belongs to a menu the plan opened (the party list draws one; B would leave it). A text box is the white panel in the double black frame (`screen.text_box_open`). A fade to black fills the same rows and is not speech
- **Scripted scenes**: a walk that neither moves nor turns the player, while the world region keeps changing, is a scene holding the input (the rival walking to his ball, then to the exit). `execute` sends `wait_30` rounds, up to 10, until the world region is still for two rounds, then presses that walk once more. A text box, a battle, or a map change during the wait ends the burst there. A still picture after the first round is a wall: it costs 30 frames and no second press. Each planner look during those scenes used to buy 20 frames
- **Menus and prompts**: fixed starter, nickname, healing, candy, and deposit menus are handled by preparation code; shop.py owns Mart transactions. Other active ▶ menus (NEW GAME, name lists, START, and unhandled prompts) go to Jev as one option per row; code moves the highlight and presses A. Hollow ▷ marks an inactive menu, not the current cursor. A row the goal names is tagged "the goal names this row". Name lists offer no B. An unsure answer at a YES/NO goes to System 2, because B there answers NO. Paging stops at a prompt by itself; the *next* B answers it
- Action **`skip_dialog`**: agent-requested mash through narrative text, **B for overworld speech**. Each overworld round is `hold_b_30` then `wait_30` released, so every round is a new press (pitfall #13). It stops when the box closes, when the whole frame stops changing for two rounds (max 6 rounds; the blinking ▼ is masked out), when a prompt appears, or when naming locks. The world region counts. In battle the macro sends one `press_a` + `wait_30` and returns, so a final battle page cannot hold B into evolution. Each round rechecks decoded evolution text before any input. No walks after it in the same burst, and no walk is pressed while a box is open — the D-pad does nothing until it closes. If a `skip_dialog` left an open box unchanged, the next cycle takes a System 2 look and presses its button; in battle it presses A instead (A only advances battle text), and the level-up stats box always gets A. It does not page the Pokédex page before the starter's YES/NO (bit 6 is set there, pitfall #4); A turns that page. `a_until_dialog_end` is served by the same macro (pitfall #5)
- **Holds and A**: `execute` sends `wait_12` released after every `hold_*`, and `wait_30` after a burst's last `press_a`, so the look after it sees the box A opened (pitfall #14). A walk that only turns the player stops the path, unless it turned toward that walk's direction and the next step is `press_a`: then that A goes out, since it is what the turn was for (`walk_up` into the table, then A on the ball)
- Multi-tile walks: `walk_up_3` etc. expand client-side before `/action`

### Vision

- Screenshots: native **160×144** PNG from atomic `GET /snapshot` for runtime pixel checks (digests, text box, prompt box); standalone `GET /screenshot` remains available
- Vision calls (planner, Recovery, cursor-mode roles) instead attach pokemon-agent's `GET /screenshot/grid?scale=4`: the same frame at **640×576** with labelled A1..J9 walk cells and the player boxed at E5. It is fetched only for a vision call (`NousRedEnvironment.vision_frame`) and falls back to the native frame on an older server. The prompts tell the model each cell is one walk tile; the `map.grid` ASCII uses the same cells
- Attached via Cursor `SDKImage.from_file` with the file's real pixel size as `dimension` metadata
- **No Gemini `media_resolution`** (low/medium/high) on this SDK path — vision-only does not change image token billing by itself
- `vision_only` default **true**: no RAM JSON goes to the vision director, and nothing gates input on `joy_ignore`/`dialog_active` (pitfall #1). System 1 is text-only and does read RAM that describes what is drawn or placed: the tilemap text (`wTileMap`, framed menus plus explicit battle/party parsers; speech requires the pixel text-box check), the warp/sign/sprite tables, map size, and edge connections (`GET /map/objects`, pitfall #18), and the cutscene flags (`wJoyIgnore`, `wStatusFlags5` bit 7; pitfall #22). The referee reads badges, party, and battle
- Cursor provider creates its session on the first required look in dual mode and keeps one durable agent; model errors pause the run with a checkpoint until START retries. When context hits ``compact_at_tokens`` (default 250k) it asks for a short session summary, then recreates with that summary carried forward
- Dual mode (`config/agents.yaml`) is System 1 + System 2 (see Runtime loop and System 1 below). System 1 always runs first. System 2's `steps` are pressed only when System 1 cannot act (the naming keyboard, a screen it cannot read) or is about to ask for another look (pitfall #32): as a burst in the overworld, one button per cycle in menus and naming. Battles are System 1's too (`agents/battle.py`), with System 2's plan for trainer battles
- Walk grid trust: `map.grid` (pokemon-agent's tilemap read, vanilla pokered collision tables) feeds System 1's paths (and its remembered map), but it is not "the authority". On Red Star's Oak's Lab it drew all four neighbors as `#` while the player walked freely. `GridTrust` counts each walk in a burst that lands on a tile the grid called `#` on the same map; after two, that map's grid is withheld from prompts and paths. Eight corroborating open-tile walks restore trust; another wall contradiction resets that confirmation count. A grid that shows the player boxed in is ignored when warp/NPC evidence does not explain it. Door mats read `#` too, but walking down from one changes the map, so that never counts; the warp table says where the doors are (pitfall #18)

### Obstacles on a mandatory healing route

Center briefings report observed full living HP, OK status and restored observed PP so the planner can continue after text/menus close without opening STATS. This fact does not waive a mandatory nurse-acceptance stage.

Healing keeps the dashboard primary locked to the Center/Mom objective. The `healing` walkthrough fragment permits necessary route and field-move subgoals; ordinary story-stage and stuck-story excerpts are suppressed while that beat is active. A healing exit with no complete observed path requests a planner look before selecting an arbitrary partial route. System 2's route or buttons-only plan can then supply the immediate target without being overwritten by the Center destination.
